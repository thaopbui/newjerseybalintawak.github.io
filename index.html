The Art

Balintawak Cuentada Arnis

Originating from Cebu, Philippines, the Balintawak Cuentada method was developed by Grandmaster Bobby Taboada as he taught the art around the world — drawing on his upbringing in the original Balintawak Club under Grandmasters Teofilo Velez, Atty. Jose Villasin, and the Club’s other instructors.

“Cuentada” means counter for counter — the philosophy at the heart of GM Bobby’s approach to training.

Beginning with the foundation of solo drills for speed, power, and control, practitioners progress to partner training, with increasing layers of free flow as they gain experience and skill.

Curriculum

Six Interconnected Levels

01
  • Formal bow
  • 12 basic strikes, in control
  • 12 basic strikes, full power
  • Shadow drill form
  • Basic blocks (side to side)
  • Basic blocks (high, low, center)
02
  • 12 blocks & counter, in control
  • 12 blocks & counter, without control
  • Semi hitting — one strike, block & counter
  • Semi hitting — two strikes, block & counter
  • Semi hitting — three strikes, block & counter
03
  • Grouping system with variations
  • Lifting and clearing
  • Lifting and clearing combined with head movement
  • Preparation for body flexibility
  • Preparation for speed & reflexes
  • Defense against punches while in partner drill motion
  • Proficiency in Level 2 while in play
04
  • Butting techniques
  • Pushing and pulling
  • Application of butting, pushing & pulling in play
  • Power strike combination 1 — right side
  • Power strike combination 1 — left side
  • Power strike combination 2 — right side
  • Power strike combination 2 — left side
  • Able to teach Levels 1 & 2
05
  • 12 forehand disarm techniques
  • 12 backhand disarm techniques
  • Application of disarms in play
  • Able to teach Levels 1–4
06
  • 12 semi-advanced combination techniques
  • Incorporate 10 follow-up strikes, minimum
  • Applications within the grouping system, followed by strikes
  • Able to teach Levels 1–6
