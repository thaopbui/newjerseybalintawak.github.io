About

Grandmaster Bobby Taboada

Head of the System

Welcome to the world of Filipino martial arts, where skill, discipline, and a deep understanding of traditional Arnis practice converge. In this realm of warriors, one name stands out as a true living legend: Grandmaster Bobby Taboada. With an illustrious career spanning decades, GM Taboada has become a revered figure, renowned for his mastery of Balintawak Cuentada Arnis and his invaluable contributions to the martial arts community.

Born and raised in Cebu, Philippines, Bobby began his journey in the martial arts at a very young age. His passion and talent led him to train under the tutelage of Grandmaster Teofilo Velez, one of the original members of the Balintawak Arnis Club. Under GM Velez’s guidance, GM Taboada honed his skills, immersing himself in the intricacies of this dynamic and highly effective traditional martial art practice.

After years of dedicated training and teaching, GM Taboada ventured beyond the shores of his homeland, sharing his expertise with eager students across the globe, starting in New Zealand, and then beyond. From the United States to Europe, Asia to Australia, his teachings have reached countless individuals hungry to learn the art of Balintawak. Throughout his decades of teaching worldwide, GM Bobby has also gained the respect and admiration of many of the great martial artists of the past century — including the late great Wally Jay, Bill “Superfoot” Wallace, and Dan Inosanto.

Having now settled in the United States for over 30 years, GM Bobby continues to be a shining inspiration and example of martial arts and maintains a strong relationship with his students and instructors who represent him.

Guro Frank Zhong

New Jersey Balintawak Founder

New Jersey Balintawak was founded by FQI (Fully Qualified Instructor) Frank Zhong. With over 25 years of martial arts experience and extensive training in the art of Balintawak Cuentada under Guro Jeff Soriano, Guro Frank has been presented with his Instructor Certificate by GM Bobby Taboada. Guro Frank is dedicated to sharing his knowledge and passion with students of all levels.

Here at New Jersey Balintawak, we offer a dynamic and supportive learning environment where students can develop their skills, improve their fitness, and learn practical body mechanics and coordination. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced martial artist, we invite you to join our community and discover the power and excitement of Balintawak Arnis Cuentada.

Our Mission

Building Community Through Martial Arts

At New Jersey Balintawak, our core mission is building genuine connection through martial arts. Our training floor brings together complete beginners and martial artists with decades of experience across diverse disciplines. By supporting each other’s growth, we develop stronger movement, functional fitness, and martial arts training. We strive to embody these values in every class we teach.
