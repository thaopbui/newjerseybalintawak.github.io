Balintawak World Camp 2023

Each year, we have our World Camp in the late fall near the home of Grandmaster Bobby Taboada. This year, on November 3–5, the event was held in Hickory, a town in North Carolina and home to many students of GM Bobby. With visitors from the different coasts of the USA, as well as Europe and Australia, it was a tremendous stage to debut the second volume of GM Bobby’s book, written by long time student and friend Dr. Garth Dicker.
